# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary V1 user is the project owner, using Quantrade as a private daily research companion for US equities. Future beta users should be able to understand the application without quantitative-finance expertise.

The primary workflow is simple: open the app, understand the current market context, identify a short set of opportunities, inspect one stock's quantitative evidence and risk, then decide whether it deserves further research. Users are not asked to operate a complex screener or interpret a wall of indicators.

## Product Purpose

Quantrade turns validated market and fundamental research into an understandable, dated quantitative view of a stock. It answers what is happening, why the model sees it that way, and what risk or uncertainty accompanies the signal.

Success means that the user can make a better research decision in minutes, while still being able to inspect the underlying evidence, data freshness, and model version when needed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, confident, legible.

The interface feels like a thoughtful personal investing companion: direct, modern, and reassuring without becoming casual or overpromising. It is deliberately spacious and chart-led. It communicates uncertainty plainly and never pretends that a quantitative signal is certainty.

## Anti-references

- Complicated stock screeners with dense filter panels, endless columns, and indicator overload.
- Generic trading terminals, crypto dashboards, or finance products that use neon-on-black spectacle as their identity.
- Literal copies of Wealthsimple. Quantrade should borrow its clarity and interaction calm, not its brand assets, copy, or proprietary interface details.
- Decorative glassmorphism, gradient text, and repeated hero-metric cards.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision, then reveal the evidence.
2. Give every screen one clear job.
3. Make quantitative information understandable before making it comprehensive.
4. Use calm hierarchy, honest uncertainty, and familiar controls to earn trust.
5. Treat model provenance, data freshness, and risk as product information, not footnotes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect operating-system reduced-motion preferences. Never communicate gain, loss, risk, selection, or validation state through color alone. All primary workflows must support keyboard navigation, clear focus states, semantic labels, readable chart alternatives, and sufficient text contrast.
