# Uncertainty and Disclaimer Review

## Product posture

Quantrade is a private, research-only quantitative product. A score is a dated
summary of model inputs, not investment advice, a prediction, a probability,
or a trade instruction.

## Visible context

- The home, rankings, and stock-detail routes show a research-context notice
  beside the material that may influence a next research step.
- Published score views state the research date, score scale, model version or
  model route, rank context, data cutoff, and Tier B capability where data is
  available.
- Tier B is described as having unverified historical constituent and delisting
  coverage.
- Missing data produces an explicit unavailable state. The product never fills
  a research screen with example market data or illustrative charts.

## Copy rules verified

- The interface says research score, research candidate, evidence, and limits.
- It does not use buy, sell, trade, expected-return, or guaranteed-outcome
  language.
- Signals and ranks are presented as context for further investigation, not a
  recommendation.

## Remaining review before external beta

Recheck every user-facing phrase when live provider coverage, model status, or
data capability changes. Legal review is required before treating this private
beta as a public product.
